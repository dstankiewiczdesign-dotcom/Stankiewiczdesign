Interior concept

Marble Kitchen

A monolithic island in deep green marble, matte black joinery and a single direction of light.

The island is one volume. No visible joints, no handles, no plinth: a mass that reads as a single block of stone. Around it, matte black joinery disappears into the wall, and light falls from one side only.

Material palette

Green marble · Matte black · Microcement · Steel · Smoked glass

Mood

Monolith · Depth · Low light · Mass

The idea

I wanted to build the kitchen around one decisive element: an island that reads as a monolithic block of deep green marble. Its veining and proportions set the character of the whole space. The other surfaces form a calm background, so the stone stays the most important material in the composition.

The design challenge

A large dark volume and strongly veined stone could easily dominate the interior. The challenge was to balance the proportions of the island, the joinery and the space around them. Reduced detail and side light bring out the texture of the marble and keep each plane legible.

Scope of work

I developed the kitchen concept, the form of the central island and the combination of stone, dark joinery and the other finishes. I prepared the moodboard and the 3D visualizations, focusing on how the veining, reflections and light are rendered. The project shows the material and spatial idea at concept stage.
