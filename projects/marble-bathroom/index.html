Marble Bathroom

Dark marble, walnut cladding and polished concrete, with light hidden along every edge.

Every light source in this bathroom is hidden. Strips run in recesses, along the stone edges and behind the mirror, so the material glows instead of being lit. Dark marble carries the mass, walnut adds warmth, and the window onto the city gives the only cool light in the room.

Material palette

Dark marble · Walnut · Plaster · Polished concrete · White ceramic

Mood

Hidden light · Warmth by stone · Calm · Depth

The idea

The aim was an intimate bathroom that helps you slow down. I paired dark marble with walnut cladding, muted surfaces and white ceramics. An important part of the concept is light hidden along the edges and behind fittings, which brings out the materials and gives the room depth.

The design challenge

The key was to make the room feel warm while dark, hard surfaces dominate. The wood warms the composition, and indirect light separates the planes and brings out their texture. Daylight adds another layer of contrast, so the room stays varied despite a limited colour palette.

Scope of work

I developed the bathroom layout concept, the selection of materials and fittings and, at concept level, the placement of the light accents. I prepared the moodboard and the 3D visualizations showing the relationship between stone, wood, ceramics and light.
