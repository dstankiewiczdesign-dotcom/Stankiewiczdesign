Marble Bathroom

Dark marble, walnut cladding and polished concrete, with light hidden along every edge.

Every light source in this bathroom is hidden. Strips run in recesses, along the stone edges and behind the mirror, so the material glows instead of being lit. Dark marble carries the mass, walnut adds warmth, and the window onto the city gives the only cool light in the room.
