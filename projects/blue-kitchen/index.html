Interior concept

Blue Kitchen

Ultramarine lacquer against brushed steel, under an ornate plaster cornice older than both.

Three eras meet within twenty centimetres: the plaster cornice, a plane of brushed steel and a band of ultramarine lacquer. Colour is not an accessory here. It is structure: remove it and the room loses its centre, not its finish.

Material palette

Ultramarine lacquer · Brushed steel · Microcement · White plaster · Black

Mood

Tension · Colour as structure · Clash of eras · Precision

3D visualization →

The idea

The aim was a kitchen with a strong identity, where intense colour becomes part of the architecture. I paired ultramarine joinery with brushed steel and a classic plaster cornice. A central island organises the composition and creates a place to spend time together.

The design challenge

The concept combines three strong means of expression: decorative detail, an industrial material and saturated colour. The challenge was to give each of them the right role. The blue draws attention to the joinery, the steel brings calmer reflections and the cornice sets a classic frame. Neutral walls and simple furniture keep that balance.

Scope of work

I developed the kitchen layout concept, the form of the island and joinery and the material palette. I prepared the moodboard and the 3D visualizations, choosing the light and the frames to show the difference between lacquer, metal and matte surfaces.
