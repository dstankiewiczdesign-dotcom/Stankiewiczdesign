Interior concept

Apartment 01

Concrete for weight, black volume for tension, art as a focal point and light as atmosphere.

An interior built on three decisions: concrete that carries the weight, a black volume that holds the tension, and one direction of light that does the rest. Nothing in this apartment asks for attention on its own. The composition works because every surface knows what it is and stays that way.

The idea

I wanted to create a calm apartment interior in which raw concrete and dark joinery live alongside a sense of homely comfort. The starting point was a restrained material palette, large planes and light that brings out their texture. Soft textiles and art give the architecture a more personal character.

The design challenge

The key was to balance the weight of the materials with a sense of lightness in the space. With large concrete surfaces and black volumes it is easy to end up with something too cold and overwhelming. Pale fabrics, natural accents and daylight soften the contrasts, and a limited number of objects keeps the composition in order.

Scope of work

I developed the interior concept, the material and colour palette and the lighting direction. I prepared the moodboard and the 3D visualizations showing how the joinery, furniture, art and light relate to each other. The frames show both the composition as a whole and the details that give it its character.
