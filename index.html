Services

What I do

Interior design→

Layout, materials and light brought together in one concept, ready for pricing.

3D visualizations→

Realistic frames that show how a space will feel before it is built.

Material direction→

A rendered moodboard of stone, wood and metal chosen together.

Cinematic presentations→

A sequence of frames for a development or a portfolio.

Process

Three steps

  1. 01

    Conversation & brief

    We talk about the space, the way you live and the mood it should hold.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Layout, material direction and light, agreed before anything is rendered.

  3. 03

    Visualization

    Frames in the final light, so you see the space before it is built.

Studio

Damian Stankiewicz

I run an author-led studio for interior design and 3D visualization. My work is built on light, material and proportion, and on removing what the space does not need.

Based in Nijmegen, the Netherlands. Working with clients across Europe and worldwide.
